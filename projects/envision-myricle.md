# Envision Myricle — Client E-commerce Experience

## Overview

A responsive storefront and owner-admin experience for custom apparel and printing services.

## What I built and QA-tested

- Product and category browsing
- Custom design uploads
- Quote-request workflow
- Owner-editable product and content areas
- Square checkout and post-purchase confirmation
- PWA installation behavior
- Mobile and desktop layouts
- Branding, navigation, and customer-facing information pages

## QA focus

I checked product visibility, form validation, design uploads, quote and checkout flows, confirmation behavior, responsive navigation, and the separation between customer-facing and owner-facing functionality.

## Tools and concepts

`responsive web design` · `e-commerce workflows` · `file-upload testing` · `payment-flow QA` · `PWA` · `requirements validation`
