# Help Desk Support Lab

## Overview

A practice environment for documenting technical support work in a structured, ticket-oriented way.

## Scenarios

- Password resets and account unlocks
- Login and connectivity troubleshooting
- Device and application support
- Ticket intake and prioritization
- Escalation decisions
- Resolution notes and follow-up steps
- A repeatable troubleshooting SOP for recurring issues

## What it demonstrates

My customer-facing support background translates into QA and engineering value through careful questioning, reproduction of user problems, clear documentation, privacy awareness, and reliable escalation.
